## Suggested introduction

K. Ramasubramanian studies the history of Indian mathematics and astronomy through Sanskrit scientific
literature. His work includes critical editions, translations and mathematical explanations of historical
texts, alongside teaching and research on India’s knowledge traditions. He leads the Centre for Traditional
Indian Knowledge and Skills at IIT Bombay.

Sources: [brief-profile](https://www.kramasubramanian.com/profile/brief-profile),
